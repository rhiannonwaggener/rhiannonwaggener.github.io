Yarrow Recipes

All parts of the yarrow plant are edible but the leaves and flowers are the most commonly used. The flowers and leaves can be turned into tea.

Yarrow Tea

1 tbs yarrow flowers and/or leaves
1 c boiled water
Add yarrow flower and/or leaves to a cup of boiled water. Steep for 10 – 15 minutes. Add sweetener to taste.
