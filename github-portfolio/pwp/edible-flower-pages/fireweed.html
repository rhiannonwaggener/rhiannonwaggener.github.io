Fireweed

Photo by: Callum1234

There are 2 varieties of Fireweed native to Washington State.

  • Scientific Name: Chamaenerion angustifolium
  • Other Names: Fireweed, Blooming Sally, and Rosebay willowherb
  • Scientific Name: Chamaenerion latifolium
  • Other Names: Alpine fireweed and broad-leaf fireweed
  • Description: The plant is 39 – 118 inches (1 – 3 mm) in height. The flowers are pinkish-purple in colors. Each flower has four petals. The leaves are narrow and are 4 – 6 inches long. The fruits are pods that are 2 – 3 inches (5 – 8 cm) long. The fruits are green to red in color.
  • Distribution: The plant grows from Alaska to California, including British Columbia, and eastwards to the Atlantic coast.
  • Habitat: Meadows, fields, forest edges, and along stream banks.
