Rose

Photo by: Walter Siegmund

Information

There are 4 varieties of rose native to Washington State.

  • Scientific Name: Rosa gymnocarpa
  • Other Names: Baldhip rose, Bald-hip rose, Dwarf rose.
  • Scientific Name: Rosa nutkana
  • Other Names: Nootka Rose, Bristly nootka rose, and Spalding rose.
  • Scientific Name: Rosa pisocarpa var. pisocarpa
  • Other Names: Swamp rose, Clustered rose, Clustered wild rose, and peafruit rose.
  • Scientific Name: Rosa woodsii
  • Other Names: Pearhip rose
  • Description:The flowers are pink in color and have five petals. The rose hips are round or pear shaped and are orange to red in color. There are prickles along the stem. The leaves are green in color, oval shaped, and the edges of the leaves are serrated.
  • Distribution: The plants grows from Alaska to California, including British Columbia, and eastwards to the Rocky Mountains.
  • Habitat: Forest, meadow, fields, wetland, valley, and hillsides.
