Salal

Photo by: National Park Service

Information

  • Scientific Name:Gaultheria shallon
  • Other Names: Shallon, Taqa (in the Lushootseed language), Viqual (in the Wiyot language), and sala'xbupt (by the Makah people).
  • Description: The plant is a shrub with dark leaves. The plant is about 7 feet (2 m) in height. The flowers are whitish-pink in color. The flowers are bell shaped with little hairs all over them. The berries are a blue-black color and also have hair on them.
  • Distribution: The plant grows from Alaska to California, including British Columbia.
  • Habitat: Woods, forests, coastal areas, and along cliffs and bluffs.
